03How it connects

Every process hands off to the next.

Shop operations pass their results along: invoices to Payment, stock shortfalls to Purchasing, supplier invoices to Supplier Payment. Underneath, Accounting, CRM and Payroll pick up every transaction on their own. That's why nobody re-types anything.

Shop operationsSeven core processes from the job order to the supplier
01
Job Order
Estimate to vehicle release: pricing approval, stock allocation, job status, invoicing.→ invoice amount due to Payment
02
Payment
Collect against one or more open invoices; post to the ledger.→ Partially Paid or Fully Paid
03
Deposit Optional
For shops that take advance payments: held and applied when the invoice is issued.→ applied advance to Job Order
04
Sales Order
Over-the-counter tire and parts sales, no job needed.→ stock shortfall to Purchasing
05
Purchasing
Purchase orders and receiving that update stock on arrival.→ supplier invoice to Supplier Payment
06
Supplier Payment
Pay supplier invoices, issue remittances, keep the check register.
07
Inventory
Stock allocation, deductions, cycle counts and adjustments with reasons.
One database underneathCustomers, vehicles, job orders, invoices, payments, stock and journal entries share one record, so every screen agrees.
