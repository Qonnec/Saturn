Trust they can see, and a record they can keep.

At a neighborhood shop the customer is right there by the bay, watching. Autometrik backs up what they see with a clear estimate, approvals in writing and texts they can look back on. That's what makes them come back.

  • A voice after every visitOne tap to review. Praise reaches the team, and a complaint reaches the owner.
  • Reminders for the next serviceBy mileage and date, so the car is looked after and the shop is remembered.

Mechanic

Every job recorded. Every incentive counted.

Mechanics are paid for the work they do, so the work has to be on record. Every job, add-on and labor hour is saved under their name and flows into payroll, with no notebook and no arguments at cut-off.

  • Jobs recorded under their nameEach job they start and finish is theirs on record, from start time to completion.
  • Incentives that are never missedLabor and add-ons they found are counted automatically and carried into payroll.
  • Findings that turn into salesFlag worn parts with a photo; when the customer approves, the credit is theirs too.
  • A clear queue and checklistParts already reserved and every PMS step listed, so nothing is skipped on a busy day.

Admin staff

Collections, expenses, inventory, suppliers and payroll, taken care of.

Admin staff keep the money, the stock and the people in order. Because everything starts from the same job orders, they spend their day checking and closing, not re-encoding, and payday comes out right.

  • CollectionsPayments recorded right after billing; unpaid and partially paid invoices are always on the list.
  • ExpensesBills, payroll and shop expenses recorded as they happen, so the books are current.
  • InventoryShortfalls turn into purchase orders, receiving updates stock, and counts post with reasons.
  • SuppliersSupplier invoices matched and paid on time, with the check register kept for you.
  • PayrollSalaries and wages computed every cut-off, with mechanics' incentives, advisors' commissions and government deductions already included.
